The three problems

parser: day-first dates read month first. The shared parser reads every date month first. For a tenant whose locale writes the day first, day and month swap: 6 May becomes 5 June. Every channel, every tenant on file.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type month first. booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, and then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint says. Only the form channel, only the tenants that run booking-v3. The parser fix leaves it as is.

one-day-move: the date moves by one day. These reports are not a swap: the date lands one day early or late, or two people at the same meeting get different days (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). The desk already read them that way (desk-notes.json), and the #4471 page still files them under parser by tenant. A day/month swap cannot move a date by one day, so this is a time zone or day-boundary bug.

How the merge hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. The same subjects come in for all three problems, so all three ended in one ticket. The cause depends on the tenant and the way in (causes.ts), not on the subject.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant’s cause in both directions. The table counts by cause, not by tag.
  • The ticket export overlaps between pages, so some reports come twice. Each id is counted once.

What to change

  • Engineering: one fix per ticket above. The hint fix is in booking-v3’s date hint, not in the parser.
  • Desk: a new decision record that merges on cause (tenant, form, channel) instead of subject. 0001 stays as it is; the new record replaces it.
