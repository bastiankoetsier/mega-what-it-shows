The three problems

parser: day-first dates read month first. Fixed (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales). The shared parser read every date month first. For a tenant whose locale writes the day first, day and month swapped: 6 May became 5 June. Every channel, every tenant on file.

hint: booking-v3 told people to type month first. Won’t fix: booking-v3 is retired. booking-v3 printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then read the date the tenant’s way, day first. People typed what the hint said. Only the form channel, only the tenants that ran booking-v3. It was not a parser bug, so the parser fix would not have closed it.

one-day-move: the date moves by one day. Open. These reports are not a swap: the date lands one day off, or two people at the same meeting get different days (“Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.”). The desk already read them that way (desk-notes.json) and counts them under parser by tenant, so the table does too. A day/month swap cannot move a date by one day, so this looks like a time zone or day-boundary bug.

How the merge hid them

  • Decision 0001 merges on subject line alone. The same subjects come in for all three problems, so all three ended in one ticket. The cause depends on the tenant and the way in (causes.ts), not on the subject.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant’s cause in both directions. The table counts by cause, not by tag.
  • The ticket export overlaps between pages, so some reports come twice. Each id is counted once.

What to change

  • Engineering: take the one-day moves as their own bug. Closing #4471 did not close them.
  • Desk: a new decision record that merges on cause (tenant, form, channel) instead of subject. 0001 stays as it is; the new record replaces it.
